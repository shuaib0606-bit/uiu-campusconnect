## B.2 Controllers
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Controller**                   **Feature**
  -------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------
  Auth/LoginController             Sign in and sign out

  AdminCenterController            The administrative home page

  AssignmentController             Setting coursework, attaching handouts,
                                   handing in and collecting submissions

  AssistantController              The campus assistant page, its conversations
                                   and the question endpoint

  ClubController                   Club directory, club pages, membership,
                                   officer nominations and panel succession

  CommentController                Comments, comment reactions and comment
                                   reports

  ConnectionRequestController      Student applications to connect with staff

  CourseSectionController          The trimester's course sections, their
                                   teachers and their class groups

  EventController                  Events, registration, interest and check-in

  FeedController                   The feed, its six tabs, their ranking and the
                                   infinite scroll

  GroupChatController              Class chat: history, sending, removal, and the
                                   polling fallback for the socket

  GroupController                  All three kinds of group, their walls,
                                   materials, notices and files

  LostFoundController              Lost and found items and claims

  MarketplaceController            Marketplace listings

  MediaController                  Pictures attached to events, listings, clubs
                                   and notices

  MessageController                The inbox, conversations and sending

  ModerationController             The report review queue

  NetworkController                The people directory, connections, following,
                                   blocking, muting

  NoticeController                 University notices, including the course
                                   notices that reach enrolled students

  NotificationController           The notification centre

  PasswordResetRequestController   Account recovery, and the administrator review
                                   of it

  PostController                   Creating, editing, deleting, saving, hiding,
                                   sharing, reporting and voting, and serving
                                   private document downloads

  ProfileController                Viewing and editing profiles, and profile
                                   reports

  ReactionController               Post reactions

  SearchController                 Global search

  SettingsController               Password changes

  ThemeController                  The light and dark preference cookie

  UserApprovalController           Approving, rejecting and suspending accounts

  UserSearchController             The people lookup behind mention autocomplete
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------
